🧾 Main Electricity Bill

Enter only Main Meter Previous Reading and Present Reading. Total Consumption Units = Present Reading − Previous Reading and is calculated automatically. The Total Bill Amount is then calculated automatically using the 2-month tariff slabs, fuel charge and electricity duty.

Slab-based split method: The app first calculates the complete household bill using the 2-month slabs. The resulting Energy Charges are then shared according to each person's sub-meter units, so the three energy shares add exactly to the main Energy Charges. Fuel charges are also shared by units, Fixed Charges are divided equally, and Electricity Duty is calculated on each person's allocated charges. The final Split Difference checks that Himmat + Ashok + Paras equals the Main Bill exactly.
